01 — SYNOPSIS

The world has
fallen seven times.

ROUGHTALE8 takes place in the eighth world — a world standing at the edge of destruction. There is no ninth world waiting. Five strangers meet with no known connection to one another. Through their journey, trust, friendship, conflict, sacrifice, and emotional bonds begin to form.

The player is never given a single protagonist. All five characters are playable, and the story changes through actions, hesitation, courage, mistakes, death, and even choices that were never made.

“Every choice. Every life. Every ending.”

SYSTEM 01

NO SAFE CHOICE

There is no completely safe decision. Saving one person may mean losing something else.

SYSTEM 02

TIME

When the player takes too long, the system may decide for them. The world keeps moving.

SYSTEM 03
